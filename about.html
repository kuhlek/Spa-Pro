Our story

Since 2011

How it all began

In 2011, our founder Lerato Mahlangu left a stressful corporate job exhausted and in need of a proper rest. The spas she visited were either too clinical or too noisy. She wanted somewhere calm, friendly and private.

With three rooms, a small team and a simple belief that real luxury means time, attention and quiet, Aurelia opened its doors. Word spread through friends first, then through guests who came back every month.

Our Mission
